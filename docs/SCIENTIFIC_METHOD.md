# ARAKNO-AI Scientific Method

## Research Integrity Principles

ARAKNO-AI is designed to support rigorous scientific investigation. The following principles govern how the platform handles scientific information.

## Evidence Categories

All evidence records are classified into one of five categories:

| Category | Label | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| A | Human Clinical Research | Studies conducted in human subjects |
| B | Animal Experimental Research | Studies using animal models |
| C | Cell and Laboratory Research | In vitro studies, cell cultures, bench experiments |
| D | Computational Predictions | Molecular modelling, structural predictions, simulations |
| E | Theoretical Hypotheses | Expert opinions, reviews, theoretical frameworks |

**Important**: Evidence categories describe the *type* of evidence, not its *quality*. A well-designed cell study (C) may provide stronger evidence for a specific molecular mechanism than a poorly designed clinical study (A).

## Citation Requirements

1. Every claim must be traceable to its source publication
2. No fabricated citations (DOIs, PMIDs, or references)
3. No citations to publications that were never retrieved
4. AI-generated text must be distinguished from source material
5. Computational predictions must be labelled as such

## Protein Structure Predictions

When using AlphaFold predictions:

- Predictions are computational models, not experimental structures
- pLDDT scores indicate model confidence, not biological correctness
- Single-chain predictions may not reflect multi-chain assemblies
- High confidence does not prove biological function
- Always compare with available experimental data

## Research Reports

Generated reports must:
1. Clearly state the research question
2. Document methodology and sources
3. Present findings with source references
4. Identify conflicting evidence
5. Acknowledge limitations
6. Distinguish findings from interpretations
7. Never present AI summaries as established facts

## Medical Disclaimer

This platform is NOT:
- A medical device
- A diagnostic tool
- A clinical decision support system
- A treatment recommender

All outputs should be treated as research findings requiring independent verification by qualified professionals.
